इंग्रजी (English) (71,89,000+ articles)

Origin: English originated in England and developed from the Germanic languages brought to Britain by Anglo-Saxon settlers.
Language family: Indo-European → Germanic → West Germanic.
Script: Latin (Roman) alphabet.
Major development: English developed through Old English, Middle English, and Modern English.
Speakers: It is one of the world's most widely spoken languages and is an important international language.
Importance: English is widely used in education, science, technology, business, aviation, and international communication.
Interesting fact: English has borrowed words from many languages, including French, Latin, Greek, Hindi, Arabic, and Sanskrit.



हिन्दी (Hindi) (16,90,000+ लेख)

Origin: Hindi developed from the Indo-Aryan languages of northern India, with roots in Sanskrit and Prakrit languages.
Language family: Indo-European → Indo-Iranian → Indo-Aryan.
Script: Devanagari (देवनागरी).
Major development: Modern Hindi developed from forms of Hindustani and related northern Indian dialects.
Speakers: Hindi is one of the most widely spoken languages in India and is also spoken by communities around the world.
Importance: Hindi is widely used in education, literature, films, television, media, and everyday communication.
Interesting fact: Hindi and Urdu share much of their everyday spoken vocabulary and grammatical structure, although they traditionally use different scripts.


संस्कृत (Sanskrit) (15,03,000+ articles)

Origin: Sanskrit is one of the oldest documented languages of the Indian subcontinent and has a long history in Indian culture and literature.
Language family: Indo-European → Indo-Iranian → Indo-Aryan.
Script: Sanskrit has historically been written in several scripts; Devanagari (देवनागरी) is commonly used today.
Major development: Sanskrit developed through different stages, including Vedic Sanskrit and Classical Sanskrit.
Literature: Sanskrit has a vast literary tradition including the Vedas, Upanishads, Mahabharata, Ramayana, Puranas, poetry, philosophy, and scientific works.
Importance: Sanskrit has had a major influence on many Indian languages and has played an important role in Indian philosophy, religion, literature, and knowledge traditions.
Interesting fact: The ancient grammarian Panini created the Ashtadhyayi, a highly systematic and influential work on Sanskrit grammar.


मराठी (Marathi) (31,25,000+ articles)

Origin: Marathi is an Indo-Aryan language that developed in western India from earlier forms of Maharashtri Prakrit and related linguistic traditions.
Language family: Indo-European → Indo-Iranian → Indo-Aryan.
Script: Devanagari (देवनागरी).
Major development: Marathi developed over many centuries and has a rich history of medieval and modern literature.
Speakers: Marathi is primarily spoken in Maharashtra and by Marathi-speaking communities in other parts of India and around the world.
Importance: Marathi is an important language of Maharashtra's culture, history, literature, administration, education, and daily communication.
Interesting fact: Marathi has a rich literary tradition with famous poets and writers such as Dnyaneshwar, Namdev, Eknath, and Tukaram.


कन्नड (ಕನ್ನಡ) (21,03,000+ articles)

Origin: Kannada is a major Dravidian language of southern India with a literary history spanning many centuries.
Language family: Dravidian.
Script: Kannada script (ಕನ್ನಡ ಲಿಪಿ).
Major development: Kannada developed through different historical stages, including Old Kannada, Middle Kannada, and Modern Kannada.
Speakers: Kannada is primarily spoken in Karnataka and by Kannada-speaking communities in other parts of India and around the world.
Importance: Kannada is central to the culture, history, literature, education, and daily life of Karnataka.
Interesting fact: Kannada has one of India's oldest continuous literary traditions among the major modern Indian languages.
