Hindi Language

Introduction to Hindi

Hindi is one of the major languages of India and is widely spoken in northern, central, and other parts of the country. It is also spoken by communities in many countries around the world. Hindi is widely used in education, literature, cinema, television, media, government, and everyday communication.

Language Family

Language family: Indo-European → Indo-Iranian → Indo-Aryan.
Hindi is related to several other Indo-Aryan languages, including Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, and Assamese.

History of hindi

The history of Hindi can be traced through several stages of linguistic development in northern India. Earlier Indo-Aryan languages gradually developed into Middle Indo-Aryan and later New Indo-Aryan languages. Hindi emerged as one of the major modern Indo-Aryan languages.

Development Of Modern hindi

Modern Standard Hindi is primarily based on the Khari Boli variety associated historically with the Delhi region and surrounding areas. During the 19th and 20th centuries, Hindi developed into an important language of education, administration, journalism, literature, and public communication

Hindi in India

Hindi is spoken by communities outside India, including communities in countries such as Nepal, Fiji, Mauritius, Suriname, Guyana, Trinidad and Tobago, and other parts of the world. Hindi-language media and entertainment also have an international audience.
