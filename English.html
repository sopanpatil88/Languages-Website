English Language

English is a West Germanic language that originated in England and is now spoken throughout the world. It is one of the most widely learned and internationally used languages. English is used extensively in education, science, technology, business, international communication, entertainment, and the internet.

Origin

English originated in England during the early medieval period. It developed from the Germanic languages spoken by Anglo-Saxon settlers who came to Britain during the 5th and 6th centuries. Over time, the language was influenced by Latin, Old Norse, Norman French, and many other languages

Language Family

Language family Indo-European → Germanic → West Germanic → English. English is closely related to languages such as German, Dutch, and Frisian.

History of the English Language

The history of English is generally divided into three major periods: Old English, Middle English, and Modern English.

1. Old English

Old English was spoken approximately from the 5th century to the 11th century. It was strongly influenced by the Germanic languages of the Anglo-Saxons. One of the most famous works written in Old English is Beowulf.

2. Middle English

Middle English developed after the Norman Conquest of England in 1066. Norman French had a major influence on English vocabulary, particularly in government, law, administration, food, and culture. Geoffrey Chaucer's The Canterbury Tales is one of the best-known works of Middle English literature.

3. Modern English

English uses the Latin or Roman alphabet. The modern English alphabet contains 26 letters, from A to Z.
